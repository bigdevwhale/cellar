# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-22

The first release.

- A candlelit cellar drawn in code: a vaulted stone room, a barred window, an oak rack of four shelves, a niche with a candle, and the dream's barrel.
- Laying a bottle: name, price, link, picture and the one question, why right now. "Share → Cellar" from any shop fills in the name and link (and the price, when the shop sends it).
- Four bottles by price (vial, bottle, magnum, cask) with ageing from three days to three months; dust, cobwebs and yellowing labels as they age.
- The tasting: open (the cork pops), pour into the barrel (the bottle tips) or another week. One quiet notification when a bottle is ripe.
- The dream's barrel with a gauge, sealed with wax when full; the sealed barrels kept below.
- The sommelier's journal: a verdict a month after opening (vintage, so-so, vinegar).
- The ledger by month, year or all time, the cellarman's notes on your reasons, and nine wax seals.
- Storms on 11.11, Black Friday to Cyber Monday, 12.12 and 6.18 that double the ageing.
- A gift shelf with dates and reminders on the cellar screen.
- A guide on the first run and behind the "?". Six currencies. English and Russian. No internet.
